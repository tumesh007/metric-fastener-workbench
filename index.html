Metric Fastener Workbench

Configure ISO coarse metric sizes M1–M10, prototype screws, inserts and wall plugs, or arrange several nuts and bolts in one STL. Download the Mac app package.

GitHub Pages is static. Direct STL export needs the local Node/OpenSCAD app; otherwise download SCAD and export from OpenSCAD.

Model preview

New self-tapping screws, printed insert mock-ups and wall plugs are prototype geometry, not certified fasteners, inserts or masonry anchors. Plastic nuts and bolts are not strength-rated metal replacements.
